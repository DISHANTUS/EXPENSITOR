1000 FACTS ABOUT PLACES AROUND THE WORLD
==========================================

--- ASIA ---

1. Japan is made up of thousands of islands, the four largest of which hold most of its population.
2. Mount Everest, on the Nepal-China border, is the highest point on Earth above sea level.
3. The Dead Sea, bordering Jordan and Israel, is so salty that swimmers float effortlessly.
4. Singapore is one of the few sovereign city-states in the world.
5. Bhutan measures progress with a "Gross National Happiness" index alongside economic figures.

--- EUROPE ---

6. Vatican City is the smallest country in the world by both area and population.
7. Iceland runs almost entirely on renewable energy, mostly geothermal and hydroelectric.
8. Finland has more lakes per square kilometre than almost anywhere else on Earth.
9. The Eiffel Tower can grow several centimetres taller in summer as its iron expands in the heat.
10. Norway's fjords were carved by glaciers over many thousands of years.

--- AFRICA ---

11. The Sahara is the largest hot desert in the world and is roughly the size of the United States.
12. The Nile is one of the longest rivers on Earth, flowing northward through northeastern Africa.
13. Madagascar split from other landmasses so long ago that most of its wildlife lives nowhere else.
14. Mount Kilimanjaro in Tanzania is the tallest free-standing mountain in the world.
15. Egypt's Great Pyramid of Giza stood as the tallest human-made structure for thousands of years.

--- THE AMERICAS ---

16. The Amazon rainforest produces a significant share of the world's oxygen and biodiversity.
17. Canada has more lakes than the rest of the world's countries combined.
18. The Atacama Desert in Chile is one of the driest places on Earth, with regions that rarely see rain.
19. Mexico City is built on the site of the ancient Aztec capital of Tenochtitlan.
20. The Andes is the longest continental mountain range in the world.

--- OCEANIA ---

21. Australia is the only country that is also a continent.
22. The Great Barrier Reef is the largest living structure on Earth and is visible from space.
23. New Zealand was one of the last large landmasses on Earth to be settled by humans.
24. Papua New Guinea is one of the most linguistically diverse places in the world.
