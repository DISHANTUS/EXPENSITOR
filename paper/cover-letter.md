[Date]

To the Program Chairs / Editors,
[Venue name]

Dear Chairs/Editors,

We submit our manuscript, **"Expensitor: A Privacy-Preserving, On-Device,
Deterministic Personal-Finance Companion,"** for consideration at [venue].

Personal-finance applications increasingly depend on cloud services, large
language models, and pooled user data. Our work asks whether a useful companion
can instead be built on deterministic, on-device, offline-capable foundations
that keep financial data private, and it quantifies the resulting trade-offs
rather than asserting them. The contributions are: (i) a personal-finance system
whose capture and reasoning are deterministic and run on-device or from the
user's own data; (ii) an amount-aware adaptive reason-suggestion method; and
(iii) a reproducible evaluation of each component against baselines, released
with the system.

We believe this fits [venue] because [one specific sentence tying it to the
venue's scope — systems, mobile, HCI, or applied ML].

This manuscript is original, has not been published elsewhere, and is not under
review at another venue. All authors have approved the submission and declare
[no competing interests / the following interests: ...]. [If applicable: the
user study was conducted with ethics approval reference [number].] Our evaluation
harness and datasets are publicly available for reproducibility at [repository
URL].

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
