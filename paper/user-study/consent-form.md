# Informed consent form (template)

> Adapt to your institution's required wording. If your institution provides an
> ethics/IRB template, use theirs and keep this only as a checklist of what to
> cover. Give each participant a copy; keep the signed originals.

---

**Study title:** Usability of a personal-finance expense-recording interface

**Researcher:** [Your name], [Department], [Institution]
**Contact:** [email]
**Supervisor:** [name, email] (if applicable)

**What this is.** You are invited to take part in a short (~10 minute) usability
study of a mobile expense-tracking app. You will record a few sample expenses
using two methods and answer a 10-question usability questionnaire.

**Voluntary.** Participation is entirely voluntary. You may stop at any time,
without giving a reason and without any consequence.

**What we record.** The time you take to complete each task, your questionnaire
answers, your age range, and whether you currently use an expense app. **We do
not record your name, any real financial data, or any personally identifying
information.** You are identified only by a participant number.

**Data use.** Anonymised, aggregated results may appear in an academic
publication and its supporting materials. No individual participant can be
identified from what is reported.

**Storage.** Data is stored [where — e.g. an encrypted drive] and retained until
[date/condition], then deleted.

**Risks.** This study involves no more than everyday risk. It uses sample data
only; you will not enter your real bank details or real spending.

**Questions.** Contact the researcher (above) at any time.
